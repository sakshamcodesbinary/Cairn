# Cairn community standards

Treat participants with respect, regardless of identity, background or experience. Discuss technical ideas without harassment or personal attacks. Do not publish anyone’s private information, credentials or wallet material.

Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Apply these standards consistently in issues, reviews, discussions and product channels.

When public channels are created, the owner should publish a private moderation contact. No external foundation or unrelated project is responsible for moderation here.
